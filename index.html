Recognize a heat emergency

Do not wait for a forecast or chatbot if someone appears seriously ill.

Stop work and seek emergency medical help for suspected heatstroke, confusion, collapse, seizures, or altered consciousness. Move the person to a cooler area and begin rapid cooling while help is on the way. Do not give fluids to someone who is confused or unconscious. In India, call 112 for emergency assistance.
